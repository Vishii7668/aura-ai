Getting started with AI at work

You don't need to be technical. If you can use Excel or WhatsApp, you can use AI.

  1. Get access to an approved AI toolUse the AI tool your company has approved. Avoid personal or free accounts for company data.
  2. Attend your Level 1 workshopThree hours, hands-on, online or at your workplace. Register in the calendar above.
  3. Pick one task that eats your timeA weekly report, a PPT, an email you write every day.
  4. Use a prompt from the libraryGive the AI a role, the task, your details and the format you want.
  5. Check, edit and share your winAI writes the first draft; you own the final version. Share the hours you saved at the monthly session.

✓ Do

  • Use company-approved AI tools only
  • Check every number and fact before sharing
  • Remove names, prices and part numbers when not needed
  • Ask your manager or IT team when unsure

✕ Don't

  • Paste customer drawings, pricing or confidential files
  • Share personal data of colleagues or customers
  • Send AI output to a customer without review
  • Use AI for final quality or safety decisions

Common questions

Still unsure? Ask at the AI clinic in week 3 of every month.

Will AI replace my job?

No. AI takes over repetitive tasks like data entry and first drafts. You keep the judgment, approvals and relationships, and spend more time on higher-value work.

Do I need a technical background?

No. Level 1 workshops are built for everyone, including shop-floor supervisors and office staff.

Is company data safe?

Yes, when you use your company's approved tools and follow the rules above. Never paste customer drawings, pricing or personal data into any AI tool.

Can my company book a private workshop?

Yes. Workshops can run online or on-site for one team or a whole company, using examples from your own work. Use the contact details at the bottom of this page.
